How to read the privacy exposure score

The score reflects detected personal-data fields, trackers and third-party cookies, and connection security. Lower scores mean fewer measured exposure signals; they do not guarantee a site is safe or trustworthy.

  • 0–25: Low
  • 26–50: Moderate
  • 51–75: High
  • 76–100: Very high
